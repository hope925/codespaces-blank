Preparedness Guidelines

Your Trusted Eye on the Horizon. Stay ready before, during, and after a disaster.

home_work Secure Your Home

Trim trees, clear gutters, and secure loose items outside. Install storm shutters or have plywood ready to cover windows.

family_restroom Family Communication Plan

Designate an out-of-parish contact and establish a meeting place in case family members are separated.

warning Stay Inside

Keep away from windows and glass doors. Close all interior doors and secure and brace external doors.

health_and_safety Assess Safely

Wait until authorities declare it is safe to go outside. Watch for downed power lines and structural damage.
